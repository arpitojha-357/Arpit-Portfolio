CASE STUDY 01 · LOGISTICS & FLEET

Swift Route Logistics & Fleet Analytics Dashboard

Power BIDAXStar Schema
OTDOn-time delivery rate
CSATCustomer satisfaction
MoMGrowth tracking

A Power BI dashboard built on a star-schema model to help a logistics operation track delivery performance and fleet health in one place, so operations teams can spot bottlenecks before they affect customers.

KEY ANALYSIS
  • KPI & operations tracking: built dashboards analyzing On-Time Delivery Rate, CSAT, Average Delivery Time and month-over-month growth.
  • Capacity & bottleneck analysis: visualized Hub Processing Hours vs. Capacity with clustered column charts to resolve operational bottlenecks.
  • Driver performance: correlated driver experience with satisfaction scores using scatter plot analytics to target workforce coaching.
  • Fleet asset management: evaluated Vehicle Age vs. Breakdown Frequency by model to inform proactive maintenance and replacement decisions.

CASE STUDY 02 · RETAIL & E-COMMERCE

Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

PythonSQLPostgreSQLPower BI
3,900Transaction records
$233KRevenue tracked
73%Revenue from non-subscribers

An end-to-end analysis of customer shopping behavior — from cleaning raw transaction data in Python to querying revenue patterns in SQL and presenting the findings in an interactive Power BI dashboard.

KEY ANALYSIS
  • Pipeline & cleaning: processed 3,900 transaction records using Python, imputing missing ratings and loading structured data into PostgreSQL.
  • SQL analytics: queried revenue trends and found $170.4K (73%) came from non-subscribers, and Express Shipping boosted spend by 12%.
  • Power BI dashboard: built an interactive dashboard tracking $233K total revenue across categories, age groups and customer loyalty tiers.
  • Business strategy: formulated strategies to boost subscription conversion, optimize discount margins and target high-value shopper segments.
